Shopify Magic is Shopify's free built-in AI tool available to all merchants on any plan. It writes product descriptions, email subject lines, and blog post outlines, and removes image backgrounds — directly inside your Shopify admin. It is genuinely useful for time-saving on content tasks, but it has clear limitations compared to dedicated third-party tools.

## What Is Shopify Magic?

Shopify Magic is a suite of AI features integrated throughout the Shopify admin. Shopify began rolling it out in 2023 and has expanded it significantly through 2025 and 2026. It is powered by a combination of large language models and Shopify's own commerce data.

In 2026, Shopify Magic covers six main areas:

1. **Product description writing**
2. **Email subject line suggestions**
3. **Email body copy**
4. **Blog post outline generation**
5. **Image background removal**
6. **FAQ generation for product pages**

It is available at no extra cost on all Shopify plans, including Basic.

## Shopify Sidekick vs Shopify Magic

These are two different tools that are often confused:

- **Shopify Magic** — generates written content and edits images within specific admin fields
- **Shopify Sidekick** — a conversational AI assistant that answers business questions, pulls reports, and suggests actions in plain language

Sidekick is the "ask anything about your store" interface. Magic is the "generate content here" button. Both are part of Shopify's AI offering, but they serve different purposes.

## Shopify Magic: Feature-by-Feature Review

### Product Descriptions

**How it works:** In the product editor, click the AI icon in the description field. Enter keywords or product attributes and select a tone (professional, persuasive, playful, supportive, or dramatic). Magic generates a 100–200 word description in seconds.

**Quality:** Output is clean, grammatically correct, and generally well-structured. It follows a benefit-first format, which is good for conversion. The main weakness is generic phrasing — it does not know unique product details unless you provide them in the keywords field.

**Best practice:** Provide specific inputs. Instead of "running shoes," enter "lightweight trail running shoes, carbon fibre plate, waterproof, men's, size 7–13." The more specific your input, the more useful the output.

**Verdict:** Good for first drafts. Always edit before publishing to add specifics that differentiate your product.

---

### Email Subject Line Suggestions

**How it works:** In Shopify Email, when composing a campaign, Magic suggests three subject line options based on the email content and campaign type.

**Quality:** Suggestions are competent but not creative. They cover the basics (discount amount, urgency, personalisation) but rarely produce a standout subject line. Useful as a starting point or for testing A/B variations.

**Verdict:** Saves 5 minutes per campaign. Not a replacement for testing or a dedicated email tool like Klaviyo's AI suggestions.

---

### Image Background Removal

**How it works:** In the product media editor, upload an image and click **Remove background**. Magic isolates the product and replaces the background with white (or transparent).

**Quality:** Consistently good for products on contrasting backgrounds. Struggles with fine details like jewellery chains, fur products, or items with complex edges. For most standard product photography, it works well enough to use directly.

**Verdict:** One of the most practically useful Shopify Magic features. Removes the need for Photoshop or a third-party background removal tool for routine product images.

---

### Blog Post Outlines

**How it works:** In the blog post editor, click the AI icon and enter a topic. Magic generates a structured outline with H2 and H3 headings.

**Quality:** Outlines are logical but generic. They follow standard content structures, which is useful if you are not sure how to organise an article, but they rarely suggest angles that would make content stand out.

**Verdict:** Useful as a starting framework. Treat it as a scaffold, not a finished structure.

---

### FAQ Generation

**How it works:** Available on product pages, Magic generates a set of common questions and answers based on the product title and description.

**Quality:** Questions are predictable (shipping, returns, size guide) but cover what most customers actually ask. The answers pull from your existing product content, so quality depends on how well your description is written first.

**Verdict:** Good for adding FAQ schema to product pages, which benefits both SEO and AI search citations.

## What Shopify Magic Does Not Do

Be clear on its limitations:

- **No bulk generation** — you work product by product, not across your whole catalogue
- **No SEO meta tag automation** — it does not write meta titles or descriptions for you automatically
- **No brand voice training** — you cannot upload a style guide and have it match your tone consistently
- **No keyword research** — it does not tell you what terms to target
- **No email automation** — it helps with subject lines but cannot set up flows or segments

For bulk needs, brand voice consistency, or SEO automation, you need dedicated third-party apps.

## FAQ

**Is Shopify Magic free?**
Yes. Shopify Magic is included at no extra cost on all Shopify plans, including Basic ($39/month). There are no per-use fees or usage limits as of 2026.

**Is Shopify Magic good enough to replace paid AI writing tools?**
For small stores with under 50 products, yes — it handles the basics well. For larger catalogues, stores needing bulk generation, or businesses with strong brand voice requirements, dedicated tools like Describely or Jasper produce more consistent and scalable results.

**Does Shopify Magic write SEO-optimised descriptions?**
It writes readable, structured copy, but it does not insert specific keywords strategically. For SEO-focused descriptions, provide your target keyword in the input field and review whether it appears naturally in the output before publishing.

**Can Shopify Magic write in languages other than English?**
Yes. Shopify Magic supports multiple languages. Select your store language in the prompt or write your input keywords in the target language.

**What is the difference between Shopify Magic and Shopify Sidekick?**
Magic generates content (text and images) in specific admin fields. Sidekick is a conversational assistant — you ask it questions about your store ("What were my top products last month?") and it responds with data and suggestions. They are complementary tools, not alternatives.
