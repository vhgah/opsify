The best free AI chatbot for most Shopify beginners is **Shopify Inbox** — it's built in, costs nothing, and handles the most common customer questions out of the box. Paid chatbots like Tidio Lyro and Gorgias are worth the upgrade only when your support volume grows past what one person can manage manually. Here's the full breakdown.

## What Makes a Chatbot "AI" vs Just a Bot

Most chatbots marketed as "AI" fall into two categories:

**Rule-based bots**: Follow decision trees you configure. Customer picks from menu options, bot delivers a scripted answer. Fast to set up, predictable, but can't handle anything outside the script.

**Generative AI bots**: Use large language models to understand and respond to typed questions naturally. Can handle questions you didn't anticipate. Requires training on your store's content.

Free plans almost always offer rule-based bots. Generative AI usually sits behind a paid tier.

## Free AI Chatbot Options for Shopify

### Shopify Inbox (Free)

Shopify Inbox is available on every Shopify plan with no additional cost. It combines live chat with basic AI assistance through Shopify Magic.

**What it does:**

- Lets customers start a chat from any page of your store
- Shopify Magic suggests replies to common questions using your product data and FAQs
- Automatically shows the customer's cart during the conversation
- Sends missed conversations to email so nothing gets lost

**What it doesn't do:**

- No autonomous AI — a human still needs to respond (Shopify Magic only suggests, doesn't auto-reply)
- No 24/7 automated responses without a human online
- No multi-language support beyond English

**Best for:** Stores under 30 orders/month where the owner can check in on chat a few times per day.

---

### Tidio (Free Plan)

Tidio's free plan gives you live chat plus one basic chatbot flow.

**Free plan includes:**

- Up to 50 live chat conversations/month
- 1 chatbot flow (rule-based)
- Basic visitor tracking

**Limitations:**

- 50 conversations/month fills up fast on any store with real traffic
- The free chatbot is rule-based only — no generative AI
- Tidio branding appears in the chat widget

**Best for:** Testing whether customers actually use chat before committing to a paid plan.

---

### Re:amaze (Free Trial Only)

Re:amaze offers a 14-day free trial but no permanent free plan. It's worth mentioning because the trial lets you properly evaluate AI-powered support before paying. Starts at $29/month after trial.

---

### Chatra (Free Plan)

- Unlimited agents on free plan
- Live chat only — no AI on free tier
- Good for human-staffed support, not automated AI responses

## Paid AI Chatbots Worth Considering

### Tidio Lyro ($29–$49/month)

Lyro is Tidio's generative AI layer, available only on paid plans. It's the most accessible paid AI chatbot for Shopify beginners.

**What Lyro does:**

- Reads your FAQ page, product pages, and knowledge base
- Answers customer questions conversationally without any manual rule-building
- Handles 70%+ of common queries autonomously (based on Tidio's own data)
- Escalates to a human when it can't answer

**Pricing:**

- Lyro AI starts at $29/month for 50 AI conversations
- $49/month for 200 AI conversations
- Custom pricing above that

**Worth it when:** You're spending 30+ minutes per day on repetitive support questions (shipping times, return policy, product sizing).

**Real cost comparison:** If your time is worth $20/hour and you spend 1.5 hours/day on support, that's $900/month in time vs $29/month for Lyro.

---

### Gorgias ($10–$60/month)

Gorgias is purpose-built for e-commerce and integrates more deeply with Shopify than any other helpdesk.

**Key features:**

- AI auto-replies to order status, return requests, shipping delays using live Shopify order data
- Revenue statistics per support ticket (see which conversations lead to sales)
- Macros for templated responses
- Intent detection — identifies angry customers automatically

**Pricing:**

- Starter: $10/month (50 tickets included)
- Basic: $60/month (300 tickets included)
- Overage charges apply above included ticket count

**Best for:** Stores doing 100+ orders/month with significant support volume around order tracking.

**Limitation:** Per-ticket pricing can get expensive fast during peak seasons.

---

### Reamaze ($29–$69/month)

- Unified inbox (chat, email, social, SMS)
- AI-powered smart replies
- Shopify integration shows order history during conversations
- Better value than Gorgias for stores with multi-channel support needs

---

### Intercom ($39/month+)

- Fin AI agent — handles complex multi-step questions
- Used more by SaaS companies than e-commerce
- Overkill for most Shopify stores under $1M/year revenue

## The Real Question: When Does Paid Pay Off?

Run this calculation for your store:

1. Count how many support questions you receive per week
2. Estimate how many are repetitive (order status, return policy, shipping time)
3. Multiply repetitive queries × average handle time
4. If that total exceeds 2 hours/week, a paid AI chatbot covers its cost in time saved

Example: 40 repetitive questions/week × 4 minutes each = 160 minutes = 2.6 hours. At $20/hour, that's $52/week in saved time. Tidio Lyro at $29/month is immediately worth it.

3. **100+ orders/month or 30+ support queries/day**: Upgrade to Tidio Lyro or Gorgias

## FAQ

### Is Shopify Inbox good enough for a new store?

Yes. For stores under 30 orders/month, Shopify Inbox handles common questions well with zero cost and setup time.

### Can a free chatbot handle customer questions 24/7?

Not effectively. Free plans are rule-based and require you to pre-build every possible answer. Generative AI that handles open-ended questions automatically is only available on paid plans.

### Does Tidio Lyro replace human support entirely?

No. Lyro handles 60–70% of routine questions. Complex issues — complaints, refund disputes, damaged products — still need a human.

### What happens when a chatbot can't answer a question?

Most paid chatbots escalate to a human agent or create a support ticket automatically. Shopify Inbox sends the conversation to your email.

### Do AI chatbots work on mobile Shopify stores?

Yes. All the tools above are responsive and work across desktop and mobile without any additional configuration.

### Can I use a chatbot with a one-product Shopify store?

Yes, and it's arguably more valuable — you can train it on a very specific set of questions and give highly accurate answers with less setup.
