The best AI chatbots for Shopify in 2026 are **Tidio** (best overall), **Gorgias** (best for high-volume support), and **Zowie** (best for fully automated support). All three integrate directly with Shopify, pull order data in real time, and handle common customer questions without human involvement.

## Why Your Shopify Store Needs an AI Chatbot

Most Shopify customers ask the same questions: Where is my order? Can I return this? What size should I get? An AI chatbot answers these instantly, 24/7, without a support agent. Stores using AI chatbots typically see:

- 60–80% of support tickets resolved automatically
- Faster response times (seconds vs hours)
- Lower cost per support interaction
- Higher customer satisfaction scores

## The Best AI Chatbots for Shopify

### 1. Tidio — Best Overall

**Best for:** Small to mid-size stores that want live chat + AI in one tool

Tidio is the most installed chat app on the Shopify App Store. It combines a live chat widget with Lyro, its AI bot powered by Claude. Lyro reads your website content and FAQ to answer customer questions without manual training.

**Key features:**

- Lyro AI handles up to 70% of queries automatically
- Live chat takeover when the bot cannot resolve an issue
- Order status lookups direct from Shopify
- Abandoned cart recovery via chat
- Email and Messenger integration

**Pricing:**

- Free plan available (50 Lyro conversations/month)
- Starter: $29/month
- Growth: $59/month

**Limitations:** Lyro's knowledge is limited to your site content. It does not handle complex returns or exchanges without human escalation.

---

### 2. Gorgias — Best for High-Volume Support

**Best for:** Established stores processing 100+ support tickets per day

Gorgias is a dedicated e-commerce helpdesk, not just a chatbot. Its AI features include auto-tagging tickets, intent detection, and suggested replies. It centralises support from email, chat, SMS, and social into one inbox.

**Key features:**

- AI-suggested replies based on ticket history
- Automatic ticket tagging and priority sorting
- Macros (template responses) with dynamic Shopify data
- Revenue tracking — see how much each support interaction drives in sales
- Integration with Klaviyo, Loop Returns, and 100+ apps

**Pricing:**

- Starter: $10/month (up to 50 tickets)
- Basic: $60/month (300 tickets)
- Pro: $360/month (2,000 tickets)

**Limitations:** Expensive for small stores. The AI features are more about routing and suggestion than full automation.

---

### 3. Zowie — Best for Full Automation

**Best for:** Stores that want maximum ticket deflection without manual setup

Zowie is an AI-first support platform built specifically for e-commerce. Unlike Tidio or Gorgias, Zowie is designed to automate the majority of interactions without human involvement. It connects to your Shopify data and learns from existing conversations.

**Key features:**

- Automates order tracking, returns, cancellations, and exchanges
- No bot training required — learns from existing ticket history
- Handoff to human agents with full context
- Self-service returns portal integration
- Multilingual support

**Pricing:** Custom pricing, typically starting at $500/month. Best suited for mid-market and enterprise stores.

**Limitations:** Expensive for small merchants. Requires a minimum volume of historical tickets to train effectively.

---

### 4. Re:amaze — Best Budget Option

**Best for:** Small stores wanting multi-channel support at low cost

Re:amaze combines chat, email, and social support with basic AI automation. Less powerful than the options above, but solid for stores just starting to scale their support.

**Key features:**

- Cues (proactive chat triggers based on page behaviour)
- Chatbot builder with Shopify data integration
- FAQ widget
- Shared inbox for team support

**Pricing:**

- Basic: $29/month per team member
- Pro: $49/month per team member

- **Under 50 tickets/month:** Tidio free plan is enough
- **50–300 tickets/month:** Tidio Growth or Gorgias Basic
- **300+ tickets/month:** Gorgias Pro or Zowie

## Setting Up a Shopify AI Chatbot: What to Prepare

Before installing any chatbot, prepare the following:

1. **A clear FAQ document** — list your 20 most common customer questions and answers
2. **Return and shipping policy pages** — the bot needs to reference these
3. **Escalation path** — decide when and how to hand off to a human agent
4. **Order tracking setup** — ensure the app can access Shopify order data

Most chatbots can be live within 30–60 minutes of installation with basic configuration.

## FAQ

**What is the best free AI chatbot for Shopify?**
Tidio offers the best free plan with its Lyro AI included (up to 50 AI conversations per month). It installs in minutes and handles common order questions automatically.

**Can Shopify chatbots process returns automatically?**
Zowie and Gorgias (with Loop Returns integration) can initiate and process returns automatically. Tidio can direct customers to your returns page but typically cannot process the return itself.

**Do AI chatbots hurt the customer experience?**
Not if set up correctly. The key is a smooth handoff to a human when the bot cannot resolve an issue. Bots that keep customers in a loop without resolution are the main source of negative feedback.

**How long does it take to set up an AI chatbot on Shopify?**
Most chatbots (Tidio, Re:amaze) can be installed and running in under an hour. More advanced platforms like Zowie require a longer onboarding process due to data training.

**Will a chatbot work for stores selling internationally?**
Tidio and Zowie both support multiple languages. Gorgias also handles multilingual tickets. Check the specific language support before committing to a platform.
