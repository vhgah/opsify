Print on demand (POD) is one of the lowest-risk ways to start selling on Shopify. You create the designs, connect a fulfillment partner, and they print and ship each order directly to your customer — no upfront inventory, no storage costs. This guide walks you through the complete setup using a POD app from the Shopify App Store.

If you haven't set up your Shopify store yet, read [how to set up a Shopify store from scratch](/blog/how-to-automate-shopify-store-with-ai) first, then come back here.

## What Is Print on Demand and How Does It Work?

Print on demand means products are only manufactured after a customer places an order. The typical workflow:

1. Customer buys a custom t-shirt from your Shopify store
2. The order goes to your POD partner app
3. The app prints and ships the product directly to your customer
4. You keep the margin between your sale price and the fulfillment cost

**What to look for in a POD app:**

- Ships to a wide range of countries
- Fast production times (2–5 business days is competitive)
- A broad catalog: apparel, accessories, home decor, wall art
- Direct Shopify integration — orders sync automatically
- Competitive base prices with bulk discounts
- No monthly subscription fee to get started

## Step 1: Create Your Shopify Store

If you already have a Shopify store, skip to Step 2.

1. Go to [shopify.com](https://shopify.com) and start a free trial
2. Pick a store name related to your niche (e.g. "SunsetPrints", "UrbanInkCo")
3. Complete the basic setup: country, currency, payment methods
4. Choose a clean theme — for POD stores, **Dawn** or **Craft** work well because they show product images prominently

**Niche matters:** Don't try to sell everything. Pick a specific niche — pet lovers, gym culture, teachers, nurses — before you add products. Niche stores convert better because they speak directly to a specific audience.

## Step 2: Install a POD App

1. Go to the Shopify App Store and search "print on demand"
2. Compare a few options on catalog breadth, production speed, base pricing, and reviews
3. Click **Add app** on the one you choose
4. You'll be redirected to your Shopify admin — click **Install app** to confirm
5. Create or log in to your account with that app when prompted
6. Connect your store by granting the required permissions

The app needs these permissions to work:

- Read and write products (to push POD products to your store)
- Read orders (to receive and fulfill orders automatically)
- Read shipping (to select the right shipping method per order)

## Step 3: Browse the Product Catalog

Once installed, open the app from your Shopify admin under **Apps**.

Catalogs are typically organized by category:

| Category     | Examples                                         |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Apparel      | Unisex t-shirts, hoodies, tank tops, sweatshirts |
| Accessories  | Phone cases, tote bags, backpacks, caps          |
| Home & Decor | Canvas prints, mugs, pillows, blankets           |
| Wall Art     | Posters, framed prints, metal prints             |
| Stationery   | Notebooks, greeting cards                        |

**How to evaluate a product before listing it:**

- Check the base price — your profit margin is the difference between your retail price and this cost
- Review the print area dimensions — larger print areas give customers more design space
- Look at available sizes/colors — more options = more sales opportunities
- Check the estimated production time for your target market

## Step 4: Upload Your Designs

Before creating product listings, you need designs. Options:

**If you're a designer:** Upload your own artwork directly in your POD app's mockup editor. Supported formats are typically PNG (recommended), SVG, JPEG. Use 300 DPI minimum for print quality.

**If you're not a designer:**

- **Canva** — free, has print-ready templates for common POD products
- **Adobe Express** — good for quick t-shirt and mug designs
- **Creative Fabrica** — paid design marketplace with commercial licenses included

**Design rules for POD:**

- Use transparent backgrounds (PNG) for apparel designs
- Keep important elements away from the edge of the print area
- Use RGB color mode — designs will be converted for printing
- Test with the mockup preview before publishing

## Step 5: Create Your First Product Listing

Inside your POD app:

1. Select a product from the catalog (e.g. Unisex Classic T-Shirt)
2. Click **Start designing**
3. Upload your design file in the mockup editor
4. Position and resize the design within the print area
5. Select available colors and sizes to offer
6. Preview all color variants to check how the design looks on each

Then set your pricing:

1. The app shows you the base cost per variant (e.g. $9.50 for a white S t-shirt)
2. Set your retail price — a 2.5x to 3x markup is standard for POD ($23–$28 in this case)
3. You can set different prices per color or size if needed

Push the product to Shopify to create the listing in your store automatically.

## Step 6: Optimize the Product Listing in Shopify

After pushing to Shopify, go to **Products** in your admin and open the new listing:

**Title:** Make it descriptive and keyword-rich. Instead of "Unisex T-Shirt," use "Dog Mom Gifts T-Shirt — Funny Graphic Tee for Dog Lovers"

**Description:** Write 150–300 words covering:

- What makes the design unique
- Size guide (most apps provide standard US sizing)
- Care instructions (machine wash cold, tumble dry low)
- Production and shipping time expectations

**Tags:** Add niche-relevant tags — these affect Shopify's internal search and can help with collections.

**SEO fields:** Scroll down to the SEO section and fill in the meta title and description with keywords your customer would actually search.

## Step 7: Configure Shipping Settings

Most POD apps calculate shipping at checkout based on the destination and product weight. You need to set up Shopify's shipping rates to match.

**Recommended approach for beginners:** Use flat-rate shipping that covers most orders without losing money.

1. Go to **Settings → Shipping and delivery** in Shopify
2. Create shipping zones (domestic, international)
3. Set flat rates:
   - US domestic: $4.99 for first item, $2.00 per additional
   - International: $12.99 flat

Once your store has real order data, you can switch to calculated shipping to be more precise.

## Step 8: Place a Test Order

Before you launch, order from your own store to verify the full workflow:

1. Use Shopify's **Bogus Gateway** payment method (in test mode)
2. Place an order for one of your POD products
3. Check that the order appears in your POD app automatically
4. Verify the design, shipping address, and variant details are correct

This catches any setup issues before real customers are affected.

## Step 9: Set Up Automatic Fulfillment

Most POD apps can be configured to automatically accept and process orders as they come in:

1. In your POD app, go to **Settings → Order settings**
2. Enable **Auto-fulfill orders**
3. Set the payment capture method to match your Shopify settings

With auto-fulfillment on, the app picks up new orders, charges you the base cost, and starts production — no manual action needed.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Mistake 1: Too broad a niche.** "Custom shirts" is not a niche. "Vintage-style shirts for classic rock fans" is.

**Mistake 2: Poor design quality.** Blurry or low-resolution uploads result in poor print quality, returns, and bad reviews. Always preview at 100% zoom before publishing.

**Mistake 3: Ignoring shipping expectations.** POD ships slower than Amazon. Set realistic expectations in your product description (5–10 business days is typical). Customers who are surprised by delivery times leave bad reviews.

**Mistake 4: Underpricing.** A $15 t-shirt after $9.50 base cost and $4.99 shipping leaves you with $0.51. Price for margin, not just to beat competitors.

## FAQ

### Are POD apps free to use?

Most POD apps have no monthly subscription fee — you pay the base product cost when an order is placed. Compare a few apps on the Shopify App Store before committing to one.

### How long does POD fulfillment take to ship?

Production typically takes 2–5 business days. Shipping time depends on location — typically 3–7 days for the US, 7–14 days international.

### Can I sell POD products alongside regular products?

Yes. A POD app only handles the products you designate as POD. Other products in your Shopify store can be fulfilled however you choose.

### Do I need a business license to run a POD store?

Requirements vary by country. In the US, you generally need to register as a sole proprietor or LLC once you start generating income. Consult a local accountant for specifics.

### What happens if a customer receives a damaged product?

Most POD apps offer a quality guarantee — report the issue through the app within 30 days and they'll typically reprint and reship at no cost. Check the specific policy for the app you use.

### Can I use my own branding on POD packages?

Many POD apps support custom packing slips and branded inserts. Check the white-label options in your chosen app's settings.
