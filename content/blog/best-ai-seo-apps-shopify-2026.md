The best AI SEO apps for Shopify in 2026 are **SEO King** (best for automation at scale), **Plug In SEO** (best for auditing and fixing issues), and **TinyIMG** (best for image SEO). These tools automate the most time-consuming SEO tasks — writing meta tags, generating alt text, and fixing technical issues — across your entire catalogue.

## The Biggest SEO Problems Shopify Stores Face

Most Shopify SEO problems are not technical mysteries. They are scale problems:

- **Missing meta titles and descriptions** on product and collection pages
- **No alt text** on product images
- **Duplicate content** from tag pages and pagination
- **Slow page speed** from unoptimised images
- **Thin product descriptions** that provide no ranking signal

AI SEO apps solve most of these automatically. You set up rules once, and the app applies them across thousands of pages.

## Best AI SEO Apps for Shopify

### 1. SEO King — Best for Bulk Automation

**Best for:** Stores with large catalogues that need SEO applied at scale

SEO King is one of the most capable SEO automation apps on the Shopify App Store. It uses AI to generate meta titles, meta descriptions, image alt text, and JSON-LD schema across your entire store in bulk.

**Key features:**

- AI-generated meta titles and descriptions using product attributes
- Bulk alt text generation for all product images
- Automated JSON-LD structured data (Product, BreadcrumbList, Organization)
- Broken link detection and redirect management
- Google Search Console integration
- SEO audit with issue prioritisation

**How to set up bulk meta generation:**

1. Install SEO King and connect your store
2. Go to **Templates → Meta Title** and create a template using variables like `{product.title}`, `{product.vendor}`, `{product.type}`
3. Enable the template and apply to all products
4. Repeat for meta descriptions
5. Run the **Alt Text** module using a similar template
6. Schedule weekly scans to catch new products automatically

**Pricing:**

- Free plan: up to 50 products
- Basic: $9.99/month
- Professional: $29.99/month (unlimited products)
- Business: $49.99/month (all features)

### 2. Plug In SEO — Best for Auditing and Ongoing Monitoring

**Best for:** Stores that want continuous SEO monitoring and actionable fixes

Plug In SEO focuses on finding and fixing SEO problems rather than pure automation. It runs regular audits of your store and surfaces issues with clear instructions on how to fix them. The AI-powered version adds keyword suggestions and content recommendations.

**Key features:**

- Automated SEO audit (checks meta tags, headings, broken links, speed, schema)
- Keyword suggestions based on your product catalogue
- Blog post SEO checker with real-time feedback
- Speed test with specific improvement recommendations
- Email alerts when new issues are detected

**Pricing:**

- Free plan: basic audit, limited features
- Plus: $29.99/month (full automation + AI suggestions)

**Limitations:** Less powerful for bulk generation than SEO King. Better as a monitoring and diagnostic tool than a one-time fix.

### 3. TinyIMG — Best for Image SEO

**Best for:** Any store with unoptimised product images

Images are the biggest contributor to slow Shopify page speeds, and slow pages rank lower. TinyIMG compresses images and generates alt text automatically — two tasks that most store owners skip entirely.

**Key features:**

- Automatic image compression on upload (lossy or lossless)
- AI-generated alt text for all product images
- Bulk compression of existing image library
- Page speed score tracking
- Broken image detection

**Pricing:**

- Free plan: 50 images/month
- Basic: $9.99/month (1,000 images/month)
- Pro: $19.99/month (unlimited)

**Why image SEO matters:** Google uses alt text as a ranking signal for image search and as a relevance indicator for the page. Stores with thousands of images and no alt text are leaving significant SEO value on the table.

## What AI SEO Apps Cannot Do

AI SEO apps automate technical and on-page tasks. They do not:

- **Build backlinks** — you still need outreach or content marketing
- **Create original content** — they generate templates, not genuinely useful articles
- **Fix your site architecture** — if your navigation is confusing, an app cannot reorganise it
- **Replace keyword research** — you still need to understand what your customers search for

Use AI apps to handle execution, but keep the strategy — which keywords to target, which pages to prioritise — in your own hands.

## The Right SEO App Stack for Shopify

For most stores, two apps cover the main needs:

- **SEO King** (or Smart SEO for smaller stores) → handles meta tags, alt text, schema
- **TinyIMG** → handles image compression and speed

Add **Plug In SEO** if you want ongoing monitoring and alerts rather than one-time fixes.

## FAQ

**Do Shopify SEO apps really work?**
Yes, for technical and on-page SEO tasks. Apps like SEO King and Plug In SEO consistently help stores fix missing meta tags, poor alt text, and schema issues — all of which affect rankings. They do not replace content quality or backlinks, but they ensure your technical foundation is solid.

**What is the best free SEO app for Shopify?**
Plug In SEO has the most useful free plan for auditing. SEO King's free plan is best for actually fixing issues on smaller catalogues (up to 50 products).

**Does Shopify have built-in SEO tools?**
Shopify includes basic SEO fields (meta title, meta description, URL handle) for all products, collections, and pages. It also auto-generates a sitemap.xml. However, it does not automate content generation, alt text, or schema — that is where third-party apps add value.

**How important is page speed for Shopify SEO?**
Very important. Google's Core Web Vitals are a confirmed ranking factor. Slow stores rank lower and convert worse. Image compression (TinyIMG) and app management (removing unused apps) are the two highest-impact speed improvements for most Shopify stores.

**Will installing multiple SEO apps cause conflicts?**
Sometimes. Two apps writing meta tags to the same fields can overwrite each other's output. Install one app for each task (one for meta tags, one for images) and check for duplication in your theme's `<head>` section using a page source inspector.
