# Ecommerce For Dummies: How To Build an Online Store

## Start Here: 3 Things To Do Before Starting Your Online Store

Before selecting an ecommerce platform, clarify three foundational elements:

### 1. Clarify Your Goals

Answer these questions:

- Single product store now, multiple products later?
- Digital, physical, or both product types?
- Manufacturing, wholesale, or dropshipping model?
- Small-to-medium (&lt;100 products) or large (&gt;100) store?

### 2. Decide on Your Building Method

Three primary approaches exist:

- **Website builders with ecommerce** (Squarespace recommended)
- **Dedicated ecommerce builders** (Shopify recommended)
- **CMS plugins** (WooCommerce for WordPress users)

For small-to-mid stores, Squarespace offers beautiful templates, drag-and-drop builder, and ease of use.

### 3. Register Your Domain

List domain names by priority, then register the first available one. Both Squarespace and Shopify include free domain registration.

## 1. Using a Website Builder with Ecommerce Plans

Website builders combine templates, hosting, forms, and ecommerce functionality in one all-in-one tool.

### Best Option: Squarespace

Features include intuitive editor, outstanding templates, tax tools, discount generators, point-of-sale systems, and multiple product types (physical, digital, subscription, appointments).

### Step-by-Step: Building with Squarespace

**Step 1–3:** Create account, choose template, add site title

**Step 4:** Add Online Store page from the Pages section

**Step 5:** Click the (+) icon to add products

**Step 6:** Enter product details:

- Title, description, images
- Price and sale pricing
- Inventory quantities
- Product variations (size, color, material)
- Categories, tags, and organization
- SEO title, URL, and description

**Step 7:** Add optional custom elements (reviews, subscriptions, forms)

**Step 8–9:** Connect payment processor via Stripe or PayPal

**Step 10:** Set shipping rates (fixed or dynamic based on weight/location)

**Step 11:** Optional features (express checkout, tax collection, email marketing)

**Step 12:** Choose plan after free trial:

- Business: $18/month
- Commerce Basic: $26/month
- Commerce Advanced: $40/month

## 2. Using an Ecommerce Website Builder

Ecommerce-specific builders focus exclusively on online stores, offering advanced functionalities through app ecosystems.

### Best Option: Shopify

Features 80+ themes and 6,300+ apps for dropshipping, fulfillment, shipping, and marketing.

### Step-by-Step: Building with Shopify

**Step 1:** Choose theme from Shopify Theme Store (82 responsive options)

**Step 2:** Add products with:

- Title and description
- Product images
- Categories/collections and tags
- Product variants
- SEO optimization

**Step 3:** Set up payment methods via Shopify Payments or third-party gateways (Stripe, PayPal, RazorPay)

**Step 4:** Customize checkout options (guest checkout, minimal information requirements)

**Step 5:** Configure shipping services and rates

**Step 6:** Extend functionality with Shopify App Store if needed

**Pricing (after 14-day trial):**

- Basic Shopify: $29/month
- Shopify: $79/month
- Advanced Shopify: $299/month

## 3. WooCommerce — The WordPress Ecommerce Plugin

WooCommerce is the free, open-source WordPress ecommerce platform. Trade-offs include requiring coding knowledge, plugin management, and separate payments for hosting, domain, themes, and security.

### Step-by-Step: Building with WooCommerce

**Step 1:** Install WooCommerce plugin from WordPress plugin store

**Step 2:** Complete setup assistant (store details, business info, theme selection)

**Step 3:** Add products via WordPress dashboard (title, description, images, pricing, inventory)

**Step 4:** Add payment processor (WooCommerce Payments or 70+ payment extensions including Stripe, Amazon Pay, Square)

## Tips & Tricks: Secrets to Great Online Stores

### Tip #1: Design Properly

Focus on:

- Fast loading speed
- Mobile responsiveness (50%+ of buyers use mobile)
- Convenient navigation with breadcrumbs and search
- Clear visual hierarchy with accessible menus

### Tip #2: Accept Popular Payment Options

Offer PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit cards, and e-wallets.

### Tip #3: Write Well-Structured Product Descriptions

Descriptions should be clear, in-depth, keyword-rich, scannable, and visually appealing for both SEO and customer understanding.

### Tip #4: Use High-Quality Photos

75% of online shoppers rely on product photos when deciding on purchases. Prioritize high-resolution, detailed images.

### Tip #5: Display Product Reviews

Customer reviews provide social proof. Collect reviews actively and display them prominently.

### Tip #6: Implement Effective Marketing

Successful stores use Facebook ads, Google Shopping, SEO, influencer partnerships, email lists, analytics tracking, and affiliate programs.

## FAQ

**How to make an online store for free?**
Free website builders exist with ecommerce features, though limitations apply (transaction fees, no custom domains, platform ads).

**How to create a free ecommerce site with WordPress?**
WooCommerce is free, but you must pay separately for hosting, domain, premium themes, add-ons, security, and SSL certificates.

**Which is the best overall store builder?**
Squarespace for quick setup; Shopify for larger stores needing advanced functionality.

**How long does it take?**
Under 30 minutes for simple stores with few products using Squarespace or Shopify.
